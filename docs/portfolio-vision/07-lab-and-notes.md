# Lab and Notes

## Lab purpose

Lab is the most expressive part of the portfolio. It proves curiosity through working experiments rather than decorative claims.

## Lab sections

### Opening field

A short statement and a live miniature experiment. The atmosphere is brighter and more reactive than Home, while staying inside the green family.

### Running experiments

Feature experiments that can be opened and used. Each item records status, input method, technology, and a short reason it exists.

### Small tools

Useful utilities can live here even when they are not portfolio-scale projects. Show actual behavior, not oversized marketing cards.

### Archive and process

Include unfinished ideas, sketches, and retired experiments when they reveal learning. Clearly label their state.

## Initial Lab concepts

- Living Systems Atlas sandbox
- project constellation explorer
- ASCII donut reinterpretation
- cursor field or particle routing study
- a small interface-motion study
- a useful developer or business utility already built by Kalana

## Lab assets

- one poster frame per experiment
- one social image per shareable experiment
- pixel and particle texture sheet
- fallback screenshots for devices without canvas support
- optional sound assets only for explicit opt-in experiments

## Notes purpose

Notes turns technical decisions and learning into a quiet, searchable engineering journal.

## Notes index

- one featured note
- topic filters
- compact article index
- links from notes to related projects and Lab experiments

## Article page

- readable title and summary
- publication and update dates
- 720 to 760px reading column
- table of contents only for long articles
- code blocks, real diagrams, and annotated images
- previous or next related note
- related project

## Notes asset rules

- diagrams must communicate a real idea
- article header artwork is optional
- use generated abstract art only when it represents the topic and is labeled as illustration
- do not generate fake UI or fake technical evidence
- social image template should combine title, one diagram fragment, and KD identity

## Motion

Lab can use direct manipulation and responsive physics. Notes stays calm. Article animation is limited to figure reveals, diagram explanation, and navigation feedback.

# Creative Direction

## Working concept: The Living Systems Garden

Kalana builds software that connects people, operations, devices, and data. The portfolio translates that into a living digital environment. Fine system lines behave like stems and roots. Product nodes grow into interfaces. Green light travels through connections when the visitor interacts. The metaphor stays subtle and technical, never becoming a literal jungle theme.

The experience should feel authored by a software engineer with visual taste. It combines:

- the exploratory page variety seen in Praveen Kavindu's portfolio
- the small state changes and header behavior admired in Zero Zeekers
- the real operational and architectural depth already present in Kalana's projects
- the green identity Kalana already prefers

## Personality

| Trait | Expression |
| --- | --- |
| Capable | Real systems, roles, decisions, and outcomes appear early |
| Curious | Lab experiments, field notes, and optional hidden interactions |
| Precise | Clean alignment, concise copy, meaningful diagrams |
| Human | Portrait, workspace, handwriting, personal voice, imperfect texture |
| Calm | Slow light, restrained motion, generous reading space |
| Playful | Small reactive details rather than novelty in every section |

## Visual metaphor rules

Use the garden and system language through:

- branching connection paths
- nodes that awaken near relevant content
- field lines, topographic contours, and subtle organic noise
- growth as progressive disclosure
- green light as active state or energy
- quiet dark space as focus

Avoid:

- leaf icons on every component
- literal vines around cards
- cyberpunk neon overload
- generic circuit-board backgrounds
- green gradients behind every heading
- glass cards covering the entire page

## The signature moment

The homepage hero contains a coded or generated **Living Systems Atlas**. It begins as a quiet field of connected nodes. As the visitor moves or scrolls, the nodes resolve into the domains Kalana works across: web platforms, mobile apps, Windows software, business operations, commerce, and experimental tools. Selecting a node reveals one real project relationship.

This is the brand asset that replaces the current code-window hero. It is both visual identity and navigation preview.

## Success test

Within 15 seconds a visitor should know that Kalana builds real web, mobile, and desktop systems. Within 60 seconds they should discover a project relevant to them. After a few minutes they should remember the connected green world, the real engineering decisions, and Kalana's personal curiosity.
